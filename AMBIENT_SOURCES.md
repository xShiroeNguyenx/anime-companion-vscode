## Notes

- The extension loads hosted `.mp3` files from this repository at runtime.
- The original source pages above remain the attribution/reference source for each track.
- `lofi` requires attribution under CC BY 3.0 if redistributed outside normal streaming use.
- If you want stricter internal policy, replace `lofi` with another approved source in [src/ambient-presets.ts](./src/ambient-presets.ts).
